2. What this app is

"BFR Publisher" is a private software tool registered with Meta for Developers. Its only user is its owner, Maximilian Bertram. It runs on the owner's own computer and does three things:

  • it publishes content that the owner created (short videos, images, text posts and the owner's own first comment under a post) to the owner's Facebook Page "The Bird Feeder Report" and, where linked, to the owner's Instagram professional account and YouTube channel;
  • it reads performance statistics for that content and for the Page (for example play counts, reach, average watch time, follower counts and the country breakdown of followers) as aggregated numbers provided by Meta and Google;
  • it checks a video's copyright status with Meta before publishing it.

The app is not offered to anyone else. Nobody can log in to it, and it has no public website other than this page.

3. Personal data the app processes

The app processes the owner's own data only:

  • access tokens that Meta and Google issue to the owner, stored in a local configuration file on the owner's computer and never written to logs;
  • the owner's content and the IDs Meta, Instagram and YouTube assign to it;
  • aggregated statistics about that content and the Page.

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of other Facebook, Instagram or YouTube users. It does not read comments, messages, profiles or reactions of individual people. The statistics it stores are totals and percentages that do not identify anyone.

4. Purpose and legal basis

The purpose is to publish the owner's content on the owner's own accounts and to measure how it performs. For the small amount of personal data involved (the owner's own), the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in managing one's own publications efficiently.

5. Recipients

To publish and to read statistics, the app sends the owner's content and tokens to the platform the content is meant for:

  • Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Facebook and Instagram, Graph API);
  • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (YouTube Data API), only if the YouTube connection is used;
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (R2 storage), only if a video has to be hosted temporarily so that Instagram can fetch it. Such copies are deleted within 24 hours.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. Transfers to the United States rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, as offered by each provider. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

6. Storage period

Publishing logs and statistics stay on the owner's computer for as long as the Page is run, and at most until the owner deletes them. Tokens are replaced or deleted when access is revoked.

7. This web page

This page uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. The server that hosts it may keep short-lived access logs (IP address, time, requested file) for security. See the hosting provider's privacy notice: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

8. Data deletion

Because the app stores no data about anyone except its owner, there is no data about you to delete. If you believe the app has processed your personal data anyway, email contact@maxbertram.com with the subject "Data deletion BFR Publisher". You will get an answer within 30 days, and any data found will be deleted.

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Facebook under Settings, "Business integrations", and in the Google account under "Third-party apps and services". This invalidates all tokens.

9. Your rights
