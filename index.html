Backyard birding, fact-checked

The Bird Feeder Report

What's at your feeder this week, and why. We cover North American backyard birds. Who arrives when, what to feed, how to tell look-alikes apart.

Every fact comes from a named source, usually the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, Audubon, Project FeederWatch or the US Fish and Wildlife Service. Every clip shows a real bird, licensed from stock libraries, public-domain federal archives or viewers who send us their feeder footage. We never use AI-generated birds. Our narration uses an AI voice, and our posts carry Meta's AI label.
